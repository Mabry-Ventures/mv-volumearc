VolumeArc does not require a username/password account. Reviewers can launch the app, complete onboarding, and use the free workout tracker without credentials. iCloud/CloudKit sync uses the review device's signed-in iCloud account through Apple's private database.

HealthKit:
- iPhone asks for workouts, HRV, sleep, Workout Effort, wrist temperature, and respiratory rate when the user taps Connect Apple Health.
- Apple Watch uses workouts, heart rate, and active energy during live workout sessions.
- Completed VolumeArc workouts may be written back to Apple Health.
- Raw HealthKit samples stay on device. Computed recovery aggregates may be included in the rendered cloud-coach prompt only when the user asks the coach a question.

AI relay:
- Cloud coaching uses the Mabry Ventures Cloudflare Worker at https://relay.volumearc.app, which forwards prompts to Google's Gemini API.
- The relay logs request shape only (path/status/latency) and does not retain prompt content or response bodies.
- App Attest is required for production relay calls.
- Strict privacy mode redacts the user's free-text question before outbound cloud-coach requests.

Subscriptions:
- VolumeArc Pro is optional.
- Pro unlocks only the Gemini Pro coach tier and live voice coaching.
- The workout tracker, Apple Watch app, CloudKit sync, Foundation Models fallback, Live Activities, widgets, App Intents, and readiness context are free.
- Terms: https://volumearc.app/terms

Diagnostics:
- Sentry is used for crash and diagnostic reporting.
- Events are scrubbed before send; Session Replay is disabled for v1.0.

Testing notes:
- Apple Watch app is bundled.
- Health prompts are optional; the app remains usable if Health access is declined.
- Premium purchase/restore paths use StoreKit 2 and the submitted auto-renewable subscription products.
- If questions arise around HealthKit egress, privacy mode, paid features, or subscription cancellation, escalate to Mabry Ventures via the contact information on this review form.
