VolumeArc does not require an app-owned account or demo login. Reviewers can launch the app, complete onboarding, skip Apple Health permission if desired, and use the seeded first workout experience.

1. HealthKit: all HealthKit reads are gated behind Apple's system permission sheet. VolumeArc reads completed workouts, heart-rate variability, sleep, Workout Effort, wrist temperature, respiratory rate, heart rate, and active energy where available. VolumeArc writes completed workouts back to Apple Health. Raw HealthKit samples stay on-device; computed training and recovery summaries may be included in the AI coach prompt when the user asks the cloud coach a question. The user-facing disclosure is at https://volumearc.com/privacy.

2. CloudKit: the app uses the user's private CloudKit database only for training history, plans, workout notes, and coach memory entries. Mabry Ventures does not operate a user account database and cannot read a user's private CloudKit records.

3. AI coach: cloud coach requests go through the VolumeArc Cloudflare Worker relay at relay.volumearc.app and then to Google Gemini. The relay authenticates app instances with App Attest and does not log prompt content or response bodies. Strict privacy mode reduces personal context before cloud coach requests. The in-app coach explains that AI recommendations are not medical advice.

4. Subscription: VolumeArc Pro upgrades the AI coach to the Gemini Pro tier and unlocks live voice coaching. The core tracker, Apple Watch app, CloudKit sync, widgets, Live Activities, App Intents, readiness, and local/on-device coach fallback are available without Pro. Subscription terms are at https://volumearc.com/terms.

5. Apple Watch: the watchOS app is bundled and supports live workout tracking, rest/cue surfaces, and phone/watch workout coordination.
