VolumeArc is an AI strength programming coach for lifters who want more than a generic template.

The app combines workout tracking, readiness signals, Apple Watch support, and prescriptive programming (progressive overload, autoregulated loads, and deloads timed to your recovery) so each session starts with a clear next step: what to train, how hard to push, and when to back off.

Programming that follows your training
- See today's recommended workout, next lift, target reps, and readiness.
- Log sets, RPE, rest, and completed sessions from iPhone or Apple Watch.
- Ask the coach about load selection, recovery, form cues, substitutions, and weekly planning.
- Review recent training context so advice stays grounded in what you actually did.

Built for Apple Health and Apple Watch
- Read completed workouts, HRV, sleep, Workout Effort, wrist temperature, respiratory rate, heart rate, and active energy with your permission.
- Write completed VolumeArc workouts back to Apple Health.
- Keep your workout history in your private iCloud account with CloudKit.
- Use widgets, Live Activities, App Intents, and Apple Watch surfaces to stay close to the next session.

Privacy-first coaching
- Raw HealthKit samples stay on your device.
- When you ask the cloud coach a question, VolumeArc may send computed training and recovery summaries through the secure VolumeArc relay so the coach can answer with context.
- Strict privacy mode reduces personal context before cloud coaching requests.
- Crash and diagnostics reporting is configured to mask text and images.

VolumeArc Pro
The core tracker, readiness experience, Apple Watch support, widgets, Live Activities, App Intents, CloudKit sync, and on-device/local coach fallback are available without Pro. VolumeArc Pro upgrades cloud coach capacity and unlocks live voice coaching.

Coaching recommendations are not medical advice. Consult a physician before starting a new training program or training with pain, dizziness, chest pain, shortness of breath, or other concerning symptoms.
